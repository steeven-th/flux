# Flux trademarks

The name Flux, the name Flux Browser and the Flux logo identify the browser made in this
repository. They belong to Steeven Thomas, the maintainer of Flux. The code's license, the Mozilla
Public License 2.0, gives no right to them: its section 2.3 leaves trademarks out.

This policy lets people talk about Flux and share it freely, while making sure nobody mistakes
another browser for it.

## What you can do

- Name Flux to refer to it, in articles, reviews, tutorials, talks or comparisons.
- Share unchanged builds of Flux, from this repository's releases, under the name Flux.
- Say that your project is "based on Flux" or "a fork of Flux", as long as its own name is clearly
  different.
- Use the name in links to the project, and the logo next to them, without changing it.

## What needs another name

A browser built from changed sources, for example a fork, has to use a name and a logo of its own,
which can't be mistaken for those of Flux. The same goes for an unofficial build. "Flux" alone,
"Flux Something" or a logo close to the Flux one would let users believe it comes from this
project, or that this project checked it.

Don't use the name or the logo in a way that suggests this project endorses you, or in a domain
name, an app store listing or a product name without written permission.
