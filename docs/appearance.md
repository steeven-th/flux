# Appearance

Settings > Appearance lays the window out the same way for every space:

- **Sidebar**: on the left, or on the right. On the right, the window buttons move into the
  sidebar, since the page covers the usual ones. Collapsed, the sidebar slides in from that side.
- **Tabs**: compact, standard or spacious rows in the sidebar.
- **Pinned tabs**: small, medium or large tiles at the top of the sidebar.
- **Page**: a card with a margin around it, or edge to edge. Edge to edge keeps a thin margin on
  the sidebar's side and at the top while the sidebar is collapsed, to bring it back and to move
  the window.

Each space's own look, its color, gradient, grain and light or dark appearance, is described in
[spaces.md](spaces.md).
