# Previews

Resting the pointer on a pinned tab's tile shows its page beside the sidebar, live: it can be
scrolled and clicked, and moving the pointer onto it keeps it open. Tabs a window already shows
have no preview.

A pinned calendar (Google Calendar, Outlook, iCloud, Proton, Fastmail, Notion Calendar) also shows
the next meeting of the coming 24 hours, read from the Mac's calendars with EventKit rather than
from the page. Join opens its Meet, Zoom, Teams or Webex link. Flux asks for access to the
calendars only when Allow is clicked in the preview. Calendars appear once their account is added in
System Settings > Internet Accounts.
